Our story

From the range, in bloom

Beautiful things can come from unlikely places.

Where it started

After a day at the shooting range, the ground is covered in spent brass. Most of it gets swept up and forgotten. Bloomin' Brass started with a simple question: what if it didn't have to be?

Bullet jackets are made to be torn apart. When they're recovered, they come back twisted, split and curled into shapes no one could design on purpose. Those shapes are the starting point for every pair we make.

Made by hand, one pair at a time

Every piece of brass is cleaned and then shaped by hand. Some are left curled the way the range left them, and some are flattened into petals and ribbons. Each one is then paired with glass, stone or shell beads chosen to suit its shape and color.

Because the metal is never the same twice, neither are the earrings. The pair you see is the pair you get.

Why reclaimed?

Brass is a durable, beautiful metal with a warm glow that deepens over time. Giving it a second life keeps it out of the scrap bin and turns it into something meant to be kept.
