From range to ear

How it's made

01

Reclaimed

Spent bullet jackets are collected at the shooting range, metal that would otherwise be left behind.

02

Reshaped

Each jacket is cleaned, then twisted, flattened and curled by hand. The metal decides a lot of the final shape.

03

Finished

Glass, stone and shell beads are paired to each piece, then everything is assembled onto ear wires, one pair at a time.

“Nothing about it was meant to be beautiful. That's what makes it worth wearing.”
