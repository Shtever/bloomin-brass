FAQ & Care

Everything you need to know about your brass.

The pieces

What are the earrings made from?

The metal comes from spent bullet jackets recovered at the shooting range. Each jacket is cleaned and then shaped by hand. The beads are glass, stone or shell, and every product page lists them.

Is every pair really one of a kind?

Yes. The range shapes the metal before we ever touch it, so no two pieces start out the same. The pair in the photo is the exact pair you'll receive.

I have sensitive ears. Will these work for me?

Everyone's skin is different. If you have metal sensitivities, reach out before ordering and we'll tell you exactly what the ear wires are made of. We may be able to swap them for you.

Do you take custom orders?

We love them. Tell us the colors, style or occasion you have in mind on the contact page and we'll work out the details together.

Caring for brass

Will my earrings change color?

Brass naturally darkens over time and develops a warm patina. Many people love that aged look. If you prefer them bright, a quick polish brings back the shine.

How do I clean them?

Wipe them with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. For a brighter shine, use a jewelry polishing cloth made for brass, and avoid getting the beads wet with harsh cleaners.

How should I store them?

Keep them somewhere dry, ideally in a small pouch or box, away from humidity. Take them off before swimming, showering or exercising.
